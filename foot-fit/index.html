Step 1 of 2

First, a photo
from above

  1. Stand on the A4 sheetClear floor, nothing else around. Put your weight evenly on both feet, the way you stand in shoes. Feet are a little bigger standing, and later in the day.
  2. Keep your trousers out of the frameRoll them up or wear shorts so only the Dot Sock is on the paper. A trouser leg looks like part of your foot.
  3. Hold the phone straight aboveChest height, camera facing down. The level turns green when it's flat, then the photo takes itself.
  4. Check the outlineThe app traces your foot with 8 points. If one sits off the edge, drag it into place.

On your own

Seated 3-shot scan

  1. Sit down, foot flat on the floorPress down lightly as if standing. Roll up your trousers. The paper isn't needed for this step.
  2. Stand the phone upright on the floorAbout 30 cm from your foot, camera facing it. Lean it on a box or a shoe so it stays put.
  3. Three spots: inside, front, outsideMove the phone to each spot. When your foot fills the dashed frame and the phone is still, the photo takes itself.
  4. Check each shotA green line traces your foot's top. Retake any shot where it doesn't follow your foot.

Arc
Before you scan

Helper scan:
walk around

  1. Keep standing on the paperWeight on both feet, and don't lift the foot after the top photo. The scan lines up with that photo.
  2. Your helper holds the phone at chest heightTilted about 45° so the camera looks down at your foot.
  3. They walk one slow circleAbout one step from the paper, keeping the whole sheet in view. It takes about 15 seconds. You just stand still.
  4. Watch the ring fill upEach green segment is a new angle. When all 12 are green, the 3D shape builds by itself.
For best resultsA mid-tone floor such as wood or grey tile, even lighting, and no other white paper nearby. Doing it alone? Use the Seated 3-shot scan instead.
